What to look for. Item 8 is the whole review. My expectations there are deliberately stated as ranges with both ends binding — if the GBM reports a 30% improvement over the baseline, that is the moment to stop and hunt for leakage, not to celebrate. Every leakage bug I have found in this project so far announced itself as an unusually good number.

Also read the per-fold coverage. Pooled coverage near 0.80 with fold-level values bouncing between 0.55 and 0.95 means the intervals are not reliable even though the average looks right, and interval reliability is what you would actually be betting on.
